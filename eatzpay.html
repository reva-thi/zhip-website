Eatzpay

Eatzpay is ZHIP's food economy financial platform — connecting the people who want to fund community food programmes with the programmes that need funding, through a simple, transparent, and community-governed payment system that ensures every dollar reaches the table.

Three pillars

Feed. Fund. Forward.

Eatzpay is built on three actions that, taken together, create a self-sustaining food economy at community scale.

🍲

Feed

Every Eatzpay transaction directly funds a real meal — at a community kitchen, delivered to a senior's door, packed into a child's lunch box, or shipped as a monthly family food kit. You choose where your money goes. You see it arrive.

Donors, corporate partners, and community members all fund meals through Eatzpay — with full transparency on how every dollar is used.

💰

Fund

Eatzpay provides micro-financing tools for community food enterprises — helping food businesses started through the ZHIP Academy access the small-scale funding they need to grow from a kitchen table operation into a sustainable community enterprise.

From purchasing a fermentation starter kit to funding a market stall setup to buying a used commercial refrigerator — Eatzpay funds the next step.

🔄

Forward

The pay-forward engine: when you eat at a ZHIP community kitchen, when you buy from a ZHIP enterprise, when you donate — a portion goes forward to fund the next person's meal, the next family's food box, the next child's school breakfast. The generosity circulates.

Eatzpay tracks every forward payment and shows you exactly where your generosity landed.

How Eatzpay works

Load. Direct. Track. See the impact.

1

Create your Eatz Account

Any individual, family, organisation, or business can create a free Eatz Account on the ZHIP platform. Your account holds Eatz Credits — the unit of food funding in the ZHIP ecosystem. One Eatz Credit = one dollar toward a specific community food programme.

2

Load credits — any amount

Load Eatz Credits by credit card, bank transfer, payroll giving, or corporate payment. There is no minimum. Five dollars is five Eatz Credits. A corporate partner loading five thousand dollars has five thousand Eatz Credits to direct toward community food programmes.

3

Direct your credits

Choose exactly where your Eatz Credits go — a specific community hub, a specific programme (hot kitchen, senior meals, children's nutrition), a specific geographic area, or a general community food fund. Your credits are earmarked and cannot be redirected without your consent.

4

Track in real time

Your Eatz Account dashboard shows — in real time — how your credits are being used. Meals funded today. Families reached this week. Children fed this month. Every transaction is recorded and every impact is reported to you directly through your account.

5

Forward automatically

Set a forward percentage on any of your funded programmes — say, 10% of every meal funded goes forward as a pay-forward credit for a community member who cannot afford to contribute. Your generosity multiplies without any additional action on your part.

Eatzpay Giving Options

🍽️ Meal Funding Credits

Direct credits to any ZHIP community kitchen. $1 per meal. Credits are drawn down as meals are served and reported back through the platform. Donors see the meal count update in real time.

📦 Programme Funding Bundles

Pre-priced bundles funding a complete programme: $25 = one family food kit, $100 = one cooking course cohort session, $500 = one month of a senior meals programme, $1,000 = one community kitchen for a week.

🔄 Recurring Eatz Subscriptions

Monthly recurring funding from $5 upward — the steadiest form of support for community food programmes, which plan menus and staff levels around reliable funding. Set, forget, and watch the meals accumulate.

🏢 Corporate Eatzpay Accounts

Corporate accounts for CSR food programmes — employee payroll giving, corporate matching, catered event surplus donations, and team volunteer days coordinated through the Eatzpay platform.

Every Eatzpay transaction is publicly auditable

As a Public Benefit Corporation, ZHIP commits to full financial transparency. Every Eatzpay transaction — who funded, which programme, how much, what it produced — is recorded on ZHIP's public impact ledger. No greenwashing. No ambiguity. Just food on tables and numbers that prove it.

Diaspora Giving Corridors

Fund food sovereignty in your home community — from anywhere in the world.

Millions of people live far from the communities they grew up in — sending money home through remittance channels that are expensive, opaque, and offer no visibility into how funds are used. Eatzpay's Diaspora Giving Corridors are a different model: structured, transparent, community-governed funding channels that allow diaspora communities to pool contributions and fund specific food sovereignty programmes in their home communities.

A group of Nigerian-Americans in Houston want to fund a community kitchen in Lagos. A Tamil diaspora community in London wants to support a seed library in Chennai. A Guatemalan community in Dallas wants to fund children's school meals in their home town. Eatzpay makes all of this possible — with full transparency, community governance, and direct impact reporting.

How Diaspora Corridors work

1

A diaspora group registers a Corridor — naming the home community, the ZHIP hub or programme they want to fund, and the governance structure for collective decisions about how funds are used.

2

Members contribute monthly — each at whatever level they can. Eatzpay aggregates contributions and applies any applicable tax relief before transmitting to the home programme.

3

The home community's hub reports back — monthly photo and impact updates shared directly with corridor members through the Eatzpay platform. Meals cooked. Families supported. Academy graduates.

4

Corridor members vote on annual programme priorities — which expansion to fund next, which new programme to launch, whether to add the elder meals delivery to the corridor's funding scope.

Active Diaspora Corridor Types

🌍 Community Kitchen Corridors

Diaspora group funds the running costs of a community hot kitchen in their home community — a weekly programme that the whole neighbourhood benefits from, funded by people who grew up in it.

📦 Family Food Box Corridors

Monthly contributions fund a specific number of family food boxes in the home community. Diaspora members can sponsor named families — relatives, neighbours, or community members identified by the local hub.

🎓 Academy Education Corridors

Diaspora community funds food education programmes — training community food instructors, running cooking cohorts, and building the food knowledge infrastructure that outlasts any single donation.

🌱 Seed Sovereignty Corridors

Funding for community gardens, seed libraries, and growing programmes — honouring the agricultural heritage of the home community and building its capacity to feed itself.

👶 Children's Nutrition Corridors

Dedicated funding for children's school meals, holiday food clubs, and malnutrition intervention programmes in the home community — the most emotionally resonant giving vehicle for most diaspora communities.

Micro-financing for food enterprises

The small funding that changes everything.

The gap between a great community food product and a sustainable food business is often very small — a commercial-grade blender, a market stall deposit, a food safety certification course, a walk-in refrigerator. Eatzpay funds that gap.

🫙

Starter Enterprise Loans

Small, interest-free loans (up to $500) for Academy graduates launching their first food enterprise — repaid over six months in food product credits or cash, as the enterprise grows. No credit check. Character and ZHIP community standing are the criteria.

🏪

Market Stall Bonds

Community-funded bonds for food enterprise setup costs — a stall licence, a canopy, initial packaging, a market registration fee. Funded by community members who want to see the enterprise succeed and repaid in product discounts over the first season.

📈

Growth Funding Rounds

For established enterprises ready to scale — community investment rounds where local supporters become investors in a food business they believe in. Governed through Eatzpay with transparent reporting on enterprise performance and social impact.
