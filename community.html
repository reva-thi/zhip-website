Community & Hubs

The table where
it all begins.

Community hubs are the beating heart of ZHIP — where food is grown, cooked, shared, learned, sold, and celebrated. Every hub is community-governed. Every hub owns its data.

Register Your HubFind a Hub Near You
Hub Types

Every kind of community food space welcome.

🍲

Community Kitchens

Hot meal production and distribution, communal cooking events, culinary training, and food enterprise incubation. The hub of the local food network.

🗃️

Food Pantries

Dignity-centred food distribution, family food security kit delivery, food rescue intake and redistribution. Serving the most vulnerable in the community.

🌱

Community Gardens

Urban agriculture, seed sovereignty programmes, composting hubs, and neighbourhood food production. Growing food and growing community simultaneously.

🕌

Faith Communities

Food ministry programmes, intercultural communal feasts, elder meal delivery, and faith-based food education — integrating food sovereignty into spiritual community life.

🏫

Schools

School food gardens, nutrition education programmes, cooking clubs, and breakfast programmes — building food sovereignty in the next generation.

🏥

Healthcare Anchors

Food prescription programmes, clinical nutrition education, food insecurity screening, and community health worker food skills integration.

Community Calendar

A year of gathering, eating, sharing, and raising.

Weekly Anchor Events

T

Tuesday Soup Kitchen

Hot lunch service — anyone welcome, priority to food-insecure households. Suggested donation model, nobody turned away.

T

Thursday Cooking Class

ZHIP Academy modules delivered in-person — rotating through all 10 subject categories, open enrolment, sliding-scale fees.

S

Saturday Garden Session

Community growing, seed saving, harvest, and composting — for families, children, and anyone who wants to grow something.

Quarterly Festivals

🌱

Spring — Seed Sovereignty Day

Seed swaps, planting workshops, seedling giveaways, heritage seed exhibition, traditional planting ceremony.

☀️

Summer — First Harvest Celebration

Cooking demonstrations with garden produce, cultural food traditions, outdoor feast, produce auction, children's food art.

🍂

Autumn — Preservation & Abundance Fair

Fermentation and preservation demonstrations, jam and pickle market, cultural preserve exchange, waste-nothing challenge.

❄️

Winter — Community Feast & Gratitude Gathering

The year's biggest communal meal, cultural food traditions, elder food stories, fundraising auction, ZHIP impact report.

Global Federation

Local first. Globally connected.

Local Hub

Governed by its own membership with local rules, within ZHIP global minimums. Data owned by the hub. Programmes designed for the specific community.

Regional Association

Hubs within a city, state, or country that federate for resource sharing, collective fundraising, and joint advocacy — governed by elected hub representatives.

Global Network

The ZHIP global platform governed by the Global Council — elected representatives from Regional Associations with independent advisory committees.
