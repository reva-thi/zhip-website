Get Involved

There is a place
for you in this.

Food sovereignty is built by people — people who cook, who grow, who teach, who give, who show up. Whatever you have to offer, there is a way to contribute to the ZHIP movement.

Find your role

How to get involved — choose your starting point.

👤

I am an individual who wants to learn

Start with the ZHIP Food Academy — foundation level is completely free, and you can access it online or join an in-person class at your nearest community hub. Learn to cook, grow, or preserve food at your own pace.

1

Browse Foundation modules online — free, no account needed to start

2

Find your nearest in-person class through the hub finder

3

Create a free account to track your progress and earn certifications

Start Learning Free
🙋

I want to volunteer my time

Community kitchens, food pantries, growing programmes, and food delivery services all run on volunteer time. You do not need any prior experience — just a willingness to show up and learn. ZHIP provides training and clear role descriptions for every volunteer position.

1

Tell us your skills, availability, and location through the volunteer form

2

We match you to a hub near you and a role that fits your availability

3

Complete a short online induction, then show up for your first session

Volunteer with ZHIP
🏛️

I represent a community organisation

Faith communities, schools, healthcare providers, housing associations, and community centres are natural partners for ZHIP community hubs. We provide the tools, training, and resources. You provide the space, the community relationships, and the will to do this.

1

Register your organisation as a potential community hub

2

Meet with a ZHIP Programme Advisor to explore which programmes fit your context

3

Complete hub training, receive your starter kit, and launch your first programme

Register Your Organisation
💛

I want to donate or fundraise

Your financial support directly funds meals, family food boxes, seed libraries, cooking classes, and the technology that connects communities. Every donation is linked to a specific impact outcome — you will always know what your money did.

1

Choose a one-time donation or set up a monthly contribution

2

Select the programme area you want to support — or let us direct funds where they are most needed

3

Receive quarterly impact updates showing exactly what your support has made possible

Donate to ZHIP
