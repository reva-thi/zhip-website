Donate, Give & Pay Forward

Every dollar feeds
a real person.

Whether you give $5 or $5,000 — through a one-time donation, a monthly commitment, a pay-forward act of kindness, or a community fundraising effort — your support goes directly to the programmes that feed children, seniors, and families who need it most. Transparently. Accountably. Without ambiguity.

What your support does

Choose your impact.

$5

Funds 10 hot meals at a community kitchen

$15

Funds one child's school lunches for a full week

$25

Funds a senior's week of daily home-delivered hot meals

$50

Provides one family with a month of nutritious food security kits

$100

Trains one community volunteer to lead cooking sessions

$250

Runs one full community kitchen session for 30 people

$500

Establishes a community seed library founding collection

$1,000

Funds one month of a Senior Meals on Wheels route (30 seniors)

Ways to give

Many ways to be generous. One destination — the table.

💛

One-Time Donation

Give what you can, when you can. Every amount feeds someone. Directed to the programme of your choice or to the general Community Food Fund where it is most needed.

Donate Now
🔄

Monthly Giving

A small monthly contribution — $10, $20, $50 — becomes the steady, reliable funding that keeps community kitchens running, senior meals delivered, and children's holiday clubs open. Set up once. Feed someone every month.

Set Up Monthly Giving
🍽️

Pay a Meal Forward (Eatzkind)

Pay for a meal that a stranger will eat — no knowledge of who receives it, no expectation of return. The simplest and most direct act of food kindness available. From $5.

Go to Eatzkind
👥

Giving Circles

Join or start a giving circle — a group of friends, colleagues, or faith community members who pool monthly contributions and decide together which food programme to fund. Community democracy applied to giving.

Start a Circle
🏢

Corporate Giving

Donation matching, employee payroll giving, team volunteer days, event surplus donations, and company Eatzkind subscriptions. CSR that feeds real people in the communities where your employees live and work.

Corporate Enquiries
💰

Eatzpay Fund

Open an Eatzpay account and direct micro-financing to community food enterprises, diaspora fund-pooling corridors, or community food bonds. The food economy platform that makes every dollar work harder.

Go to Eatzpay
🎗️

Fundraise for ZHIP

Run, bake, host a dinner, celebrate a birthday by feeding others. We provide a personal fundraising page, outreach materials, and impact updates to share with your supporters.

Start Fundraising
🌍

Diaspora Corridors

Fund food sovereignty programmes in your home community from wherever you are in the world — through a transparent, community-governed giving corridor that shows you exactly where every dollar goes.

Learn More
📋

Leave a Legacy

Include ZHIP in your estate planning — a legacy gift that funds community food sovereignty for years after your lifetime. We will name a programme, a hub, or a community resource in your memory.

Legacy Giving
Our transparency commitment

Quarterly public reports

Published for every hub and the ZHIP platform as a whole — funds received, disbursed, programmes funded, and every impact metric produced.

📧

Personal impact updates

Donors receive personalised updates — specific to the programme they funded — showing real outcomes: meals served, children fed, seniors reached, enterprises launched.
