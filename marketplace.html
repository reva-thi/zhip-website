ZHIP Marketplace

Buy, sell, and trade
with impact at the centre.

An ethical, community-governed food marketplace — where every product carries a transparent impact label and every transaction circulates value in the community.

Start SellingBrowse Marketplace
What You Can Buy & Sell

Six product categories. One ethical marketplace.

🫙

Food Products

Jams, ferments, pickles, baked goods, preserved produce, dried herbs, sauces, condiments, and value-added community kitchen products — all with transparent sourcing.

🌿

Fresh Produce

Community garden harvests, farm surplus, seasonal fruit and vegetables — available for purchase, exchange, or barter. Surplus automatically offered to local food pantries first.

📚

Knowledge Services

Cooking classes, nutrition consultations, food growing workshops, fermentation courses, and enterprise advisory — delivered by ZHIP-certified trainers and experts.

🍲

Catering & Events

Community kitchen catering services, cultural food events, corporate social impact catering, and food heritage celebration dinners — all community-owned and operated.

🌱

Seeds & Growing

Heritage and heirloom seed varieties, seedlings, growing kits, composting supplies, and gardening tools — connecting community seed libraries with the marketplace.

🔧

Equipment & Tools

Shared kitchen equipment, food processing tools, garden equipment, and fermentation supplies — available for purchase, rental, or community sharing arrangements.

Revenue Model

The circular marketplace model.

80%
To the producer or trainer

The vast majority of every transaction goes directly to the community member who made or delivered it.

10%
To ZHIP platform operations

Sustains the technology, support, and infrastructure that powers the marketplace and all ZHIP programmes.

10%
To the Community Food Fund

Every purchase directly funds hunger intervention — meals, kits, and programme operations for food-insecure households.
