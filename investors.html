The investment thesis

Why food sovereignty is the opportunity.

Global hunger is not caused by a shortage of food. It is caused by broken systems — fragmented knowledge, lack of community infrastructure, and a food economy that extracts value from communities rather than building it within them. Fixing broken systems is what technology does best.

ZHIP is building the platform that serves as the operating system for community food sovereignty — connecting food education, food access, food production, and food enterprise in one integrated, AI-augmented, community-governed ecosystem.

The market opportunity is enormous — spanning FoodTech, EdTech, social enterprise software, impact crowdfunding, and community economic development. The competitive moat is deep — no existing platform serves this intersection. And the mission is urgent — 733 million people are hungry tonight.

As a Texas Public Benefit Corporation, ZHIP offers impact investors a legally mission-locked instrument: financial return and genuine social impact, protected in the same articles of incorporation.

Why ZHIP is different

🔒

Mission-locked by law — as a Texas PBC, the public benefit purpose is legally protected. No future board can abandon it without shareholder consent.

🌐

No comparable platform exists — food education, community food management, marketplace, fundraising, and governance in one integrated system. The integration is the moat.

🏘️

Community-governed — communities own their data and their programmes. This is not a platform that extracts from communities. It grows stronger as communities grow stronger.

🤖

AI-native, privacy-first — built on open-source, locally deployable AI that works in low-connectivity environments without sending personal data to third-party services.

📊

SDG-aligned and measurable — every programme generates verified, publicly auditable impact data connected to UN SDG reporting frameworks. Your impact is quantifiable.

🌱

Built to scale globally — a federated model where local hubs remain sovereign while benefiting from a global network, technology stack, and knowledge commons.

Structure and terms

Investment overview.

SAFE Notes
