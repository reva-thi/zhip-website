Platform Products

🏠

Hub Management Platform

📖

Food Skills Academy

Marketplace

Developer API

AI & Intelligence

Sovereign-first AI. Privacy by design.

Ollama-first local inference — no personal data sent to third-party AI. AI features enhance human decision-making rather than replacing community governance.

🧠 Adaptive Learning Pathway

After a 5-minute skills assessment, AI generates a personalised module sequence, time estimate, and learning schedule — adjusting dynamically as you progress.

🔄 Food Resource Matching

AI matches surplus food with the nearest and most appropriate recipient — factoring dietary requirements, distance, storage conditions, quantity, and timing in real time.

📖 Knowledge Assistant

A locally deployed knowledge assistant (RAG over the ZHIP knowledge base) answers questions in 20+ languages — about recipes, nutrition, growing, enterprise, and platform navigation.

📈 Impact Analytics

Automated calculation and reporting of ZHIP's contribution to UN SDG targets. AI-generated quarterly impact reports, donor statements, and government compliance reports.

🌐 Translation Engine

AI-assisted translation of curriculum content, platform interface, and community communications into 20+ languages — with human translator review for critical content.

🔮 Demand Forecasting

Predicts demand for hot kitchen meals, pantry visits, and food rescue intake based on historical data, seasonal patterns, weather, local events, and community growth.

Data Sovereignty

Your data. Your community. Your rules.

🔒 Community Data Sovereignty

Each hub owns its data. ZHIP stores and processes it as a service. Hubs can export or delete all their data at any time. No data extraction for advertising or third-party sale.

🛡️ Privacy by Design

Data minimisation by default. Purpose limitation enforced. Granular, revocable consent at every collection point. Special enhanced encryption for data relating to food-insecure and vulnerable individuals.

🌐 Offline-Capable

Core features work without internet connectivity — critical for community hub deployments and learners in low-connectivity areas. Syncs automatically when connection is restored.
