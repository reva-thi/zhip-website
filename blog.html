Quick reads

Common questions. Honest answers.

No — though we work closely with food banks and use some similar tools. Food banks are primarily distribution points for donated food. ZHIP is a community platform that builds food sovereignty — through education, growing programmes, community kitchens, and food enterprise support. We help communities become less dependent on food banks over time, not more dependent on them.

No. ZHIP programmes are open to everyone. Our food security programmes prioritise those who need them most. But our Food Skills Academy, growing programmes, and community events welcome anyone who wants to learn, cook, grow, and build community around food. Food sovereignty is everyone's issue — not only the issue of those currently experiencing hunger.

Most food programmes address immediate need — which is essential and we do it too. ZHIP also addresses the root causes of food insecurity: lack of food knowledge, lack of community infrastructure, and lack of local food economies. We work at all three levels simultaneously, using technology to connect them and make each more effective.

Food sovereignty means a community's right to define its own food system — what food is grown, by whom, how it is distributed, and who benefits. It is the opposite of a community that is dependent on distant corporations, charity, or government programmes for its basic nutrition. ZHIP works to shift communities from dependency toward sovereignty — through knowledge, growing, enterprise, and collective action.

Absolutely. Most volunteers and learners come to ZHIP without significant cooking experience — that is the point of the Food Skills Academy. You do not need to know how to cook to volunteer in a community kitchen; you learn as you go. You do not need to be a gardener to join a growing programme. You just need the willingness to show up and try.

ZHIP publishes quarterly impact reports showing every dollar received and every dollar spent, by programme category. Donors receive personalised impact updates. As a Public Benefit Corporation, we also publish an Annual Benefit Report independently assessed against the B Impact Assessment standard. We believe transparency is a form of respect.
