For Families

Food for your family —
right now, and for life.

Whether your family needs food support today, wants to learn to cook better, or is ready to grow a garden together — ZHIP has a pathway for you. Everything we do for families is designed with dignity, cultural respect, and long-term capability in mind.

Family programmes

Support that grows with you.

🗃️ Family Food Security Kits

A monthly box of nutritious whole foods — lentils, chickpeas, whole grains, cooking oils, spices, tinned vegetables, and fresh produce in season. Enough for a family of four for one to two weeks.

Every kit also includes three varieties of seeds to grow at home, five picture recipe cards (no literacy required — every step is shown with photographs), and a card giving you free access to the ZHIP Food Academy digital resources.

What is in a typical kit

🫘

1 kg red lentils, 1 kg chickpeas, 500g oats, 1 kg brown rice

🥫

Tinned tomatoes, coconut milk, cooking oil

🌿

Mixed dried spices (cumin, turmeric, coriander, garlic, paprika)

🥦

Seasonal fresh vegetables and fruit from the community garden or local food rescue

🌱

Three seed packets — one herb, one fast-growing vegetable, one legume

👩‍🍳 Family Cooking Courses

Six-week cooking courses designed for parents and caregivers — running in community kitchens in the evenings or on weekend mornings, when school is out. No experience needed. All ingredients provided.

What families learn

✅

Five nutritious meals that can be made in under 30 minutes from affordable whole ingredients

✅

How to plan a week of meals that reduces waste and stays within a tight budget

✅

Simple fermentation — making yoghurt, pickles, and a basic kimchi at home

✅

How to involve children in cooking safely and joyfully

✅

The nutritional basics — what growing bodies need, and where to find it affordably

👶 Children's Nutrition First

For families with children experiencing food insecurity — school breakfast and lunch programmes, holiday meal clubs (so children eat well even when school is out), and children's growing clubs where children tend a small garden patch and learn where food comes from.

How it works

🍳

Breakfast and lunch provided at school through ZHIP partner programmes — nutritious, culturally appropriate, freshly cooked

☀️

Holiday food clubs run during school holidays — children come for a hot meal, a growing activity, and a cooking session they take home

🌱

School growing gardens — children plant, tend, harvest, and eat. Teachers receive curriculum support from ZHIP to connect the garden to classroom learning.

🌱 Family Growing Starter

A growing starter kit for families who want to begin growing their own food — whatever their space. Includes three seed varieties, a simple container growing guide (pictorial, no literacy required), and connection to a ZHIP growing coach for ongoing support.

The growing journey for families

1️⃣

Month 1: Herbs and salad leaves in a window box. Children water them every morning.

2️⃣

Month 3: First harvest. Children eat lettuce they grew. They want more.

3️⃣

Month 6: A tomato plant on the balcony. Seed saving from the first season.

4️⃣

Year 2: A community plot. Growing enough to share with neighbours.
