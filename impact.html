Our Impact

Every number is
a real person.

ZHIP measures impact not to satisfy funders — but because communities deserve to know whether what they are building is working. Our impact data is publicly available, independently verified, and connected to the UN Sustainable Development Goals that frame the global commitment to ending hunger.

What we measure

The metrics that tell the truth about hunger.

🍲

Meals served

Every hot meal served at a ZHIP community kitchen is counted. Every family food box distributed. Every emergency meal delivered.

🎓

Skills certified

Every learner who completes an Academy module receives a certificate. Every certified trainer who goes on to teach others multiplies the impact.

♻️

Food rescued

Every kilogram of food intercepted before landfill and redirected to community use. Every tonne of CO2 equivalent diverted.

💼

Livelihoods created

Every food enterprise that launches from a community hub. Every income generated from selling food skills or produce. Every job created.
