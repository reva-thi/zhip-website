The honest truth

The world grows enough food to feed everyone.
The problem is not production. It is power.

Food insecurity is not caused by a shortage of food. It is caused by a shortage of knowledge, access, and community infrastructure. ZHIP exists to build all three — in every neighbourhood that needs it.

How ZHIP works

We work at three levels at once.

Immediate relief. Building skills. Creating lasting food sovereignty. All three happen together — because relief without education creates dependency, and education without food access leaves people hungry while they learn.

🍲

Right now — we feed people

Community hot kitchens, food pantries, family food boxes, and emergency response programmes make sure nobody goes hungry while we build for the long term. These start in your neighbourhood, run by your neighbours.

Community Hubs
What is a Community Hub?

A hub is any space where your community gathers around food.

It could be a church kitchen, a school hall, a community centre room, a converted garage, or a back garden. What makes a space a ZHIP hub is not a licence or a building — it is a committed group of people who want to feed their neighbours and grow their community's ability to feed itself.

ZHIP gives every hub the tools, the training, the resources, and the global network to do that well. You provide the community knowledge, the local relationships, and the will to show up.

Our promise

We do not give people fish.
We do not just teach people to fish.
We work to change who controls the water.

ZHIP is about food sovereignty — the right of every community to define its own food system, own its food knowledge, and control its food future. That is bigger than charity. It is justice.
