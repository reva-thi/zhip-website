Giving Vehicles

Five ways to make your impact count.

💛 Individual Donations

One-time or recurring. Every donation linked to a specific impact outcome — meals funded, learners supported, food rescued. Real-time impact counter on your donor dashboard.

Donate Now

🔄 Giving Circles

Groups of individuals pool monthly contributions toward a shared community goal. Circle members vote on which community projects receive the pooled funds each month — direct community democracy.

Start a Circle

🌍 Diaspora Fund-Pooling

Structured fund pooling from diaspora communities to home community food programmes. Transparent fund tracking, direct community disbursement with governance oversight and remittance integration.

Learn More

🏢 Corporate Partnerships

Donation matching, volunteer corporate events, pro bono service donations (legal, marketing, tech). Corporate partners receive verified impact reports, platform-hosted impact pages, and B Corp alignment evidence.

Partner With Us

📈 Community Campaigns

Verified organisations create fundraising campaigns with impact-linked goals, story, timelines, and reward tiers — peer-reviewed before launch for quality and legitimacy.

Launch Campaign

🏛️ Institutional Grants

ZHIP supports grant applications to USDA SNAP-Ed, W.K. Kellogg Foundation, Robert Wood Johnson Foundation, and Texas-specific community food system grants for registered community hubs.

Get Support
Transparency Commitment

All fundraising — publicly auditable.

📂 Funds Received & Disbursed

Every dollar raised and spent is published in real time on the public impact dashboard. No hidden fees, no diverted funds.

📊 Programmatic Use

Every grant and donation is tracked to specific programme activities. The Annual Benefit Report details exactly how every fund category was used.

✅ Third-Party Verification

ZHIP uses the B Impact Assessment as its third-party verification standard. Annual Benefit Reports are publicly available to all stakeholders.
