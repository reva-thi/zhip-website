Organisations & Partnerships

Your organisation can
build food sovereignty
in your community.

Whether you are a faith community, a school, a healthcare provider, a corporation, a housing association, or a government agency — ZHIP has a partnership model designed for you. Every organisational partner receives tools, training, and a network. Every partnership feeds more people and builds more community.

Six types of organisational partner

Find your organisation's pathway into ZHIP.

🕌

Faith Communities

Churches, mosques, temples, gurdwaras, synagogues, and interfaith organisations are natural ZHIP community hub partners. You already have the kitchen, the hall, the community of service, and the trust of your neighbourhood. ZHIP gives you the structure, tools, and network to turn what you are already doing into something measured, resourced, and globally connected.

What ZHIP offers faith partners

✅

Hub registration and Verified Hub status (unlocking fundraising and grant access)

✅

Curriculum adapted to your congregation's cultural and dietary traditions

✅

Food rescue partnerships with local supermarkets and restaurants

✅

Grant application support for faith-based food ministry funding

✅

Integration with your faith calendar — Ramadan, Lent, Harvest, Eid, Diwali, Christmas programmes

Register Your Faith Community
🏫

Schools and Educational Institutions

From primary schools to universities, educational institutions have a unique capacity to change how entire generations relate to food. ZHIP's school partnership integrates food education into the curriculum, establishes school growing gardens, and ensures food-insecure children eat well every day — including during school holidays.

What ZHIP offers school partners

✅

Curriculum integration guides connecting the ZHIP Academy to core subjects

✅

School growing garden design, setup support, and seasonal growing coach visits

✅

Holiday food club programme for the school's food-insecure students

✅

Teacher training in food education delivery and safeguarding

✅

Parent engagement programme — monthly cooking workshops and a family growing starter kit

Register Your School
🏥

Healthcare Providers

Food is medicine — and healthcare providers who integrate food support into their clinical practice see measurably better patient outcomes. ZHIP's healthcare partnership creates a food prescription pathway from clinic to community hub, with condition-specific cooking courses, food insecurity screening, and direct referral to the full range of ZHIP programmes.

What ZHIP offers healthcare partners

✅

Food prescription referral pathway — clinicians refer patients directly to ZHIP programmes

✅

Condition-specific cooking courses co-designed with your clinical team

✅

Community health worker food skills training (ZHIP Academy)

✅

Food insecurity screening tool and referral protocol for integration into consultation

✅

Outcome reporting — patient nutrition improvement data for clinical records and research

Register Your Healthcare Organisation
🏢

Corporations and Businesses

Corporate community investment through ZHIP is not CSR box-ticking — it is verifiable impact, clearly reported, directly connected to food sovereignty in the communities where your employees live and work. From catering partnerships to employee giving programmes to corporate volunteer days in community kitchens.

Corporate partnership options

💰

Corporate Giving Partner — payroll giving, donation matching, and Eatzpay corporate accounts. Quarterly ESG impact reports.

👥

Team Volunteer Partner — coordinated team volunteer days in community kitchens, growing programmes, and food rescue operations. Team-building with real community impact.

🍽️

Catering Partner — all corporate catering through ZHIP, with net profit returning to community food programmes. Kind Table certification for participating business premises.

🌱

Anchor Enterprise Partner — fund and co-manage a community food enterprise from inception, providing mentoring, market access, and investment alongside ZHIP support.

Corporate Partnerships
🏘️

Housing Associations and Local Government

Housing associations and local authorities are gatekeepers to the communities most affected by food insecurity. ZHIP partnerships with housing providers and public sector bodies bring food sovereignty programmes into the spaces where food-insecure people already are — community rooms, shared gardens, estates, and public buildings.

What ZHIP offers public sector partners

✅

Shared community space programming — community kitchens and food pantries in estate common rooms

✅

Rooftop and communal garden development with ZHIP growing coach support

✅

Tenant food security screening and referral through housing officer touchpoints

✅

SDG-aligned impact reporting compatible with public sector outcome frameworks

✅

Funding and grant application co-development for joint bids

Public Sector Enquiries
🌍

NGOs and International Organisations

For NGOs and international organisations working on food security, nutrition, community development, or disaster response — ZHIP offers federated platform access, white-label hub infrastructure, shared data and impact frameworks, and the ability to connect distributed community programmes into a coherent global network.

What ZHIP offers international partners

✅

White-label hub registration and management for your existing community programmes

✅

Shared SDG impact framework and reporting infrastructure

✅

ZHIP Academy curriculum licensing for in-country delivery

✅

Diaspora corridor connection — linking diaspora giving to your in-country programmes

✅

Data sharing agreements for joint research and outcome measurement
