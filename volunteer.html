Volunteer with ZHIP

Show up.
Feed someone.
Change your neighbourhood.

Volunteering with ZHIP is not about having skills you do not have. It is about showing up with what you do have — time, energy, willingness — and contributing to something that feeds real people in your own neighbourhood. Every volunteer role matters. Every hour counts.

Volunteer roles

A role for every skill, every schedule, every passion.

🍳

Kitchen Volunteer

Help prepare and serve hot meals at community kitchen sessions. No experience required — the kitchen lead guides everything. You chop, stir, serve, and learn. A typical session is 4 hours, twice a week.

Tuesday & Thursday4 hrs eachNo experience needed
🚗

Senior Meals Delivery Driver

Collect meals from the hub kitchen and deliver to 3–6 elderly or housebound recipients. Stay for a welfare visit at each door. The most impactful 90 minutes many volunteers ever spend. Daily, weekday routes.

Weekday lunchtimes90 mins/routeDriving licence required
📦

Children's Meal Delivery Volunteer

Deliver daily hot meals and weekly food boxes to homebound children and young people. Includes a welfare check at every door. DBS/background check required. Routes typically 2–3 hours.

Weekday afternoons2–3 hrsDBS check required
🗃️

Food Pantry Volunteer

Set up, manage, and support the community food pantry — welcoming families, helping with food selection, maintaining stock records. Dignity-first training provided before first session.

Wednesday & Friday3 hrs eachTraining provided
🌱

Growing & Garden Volunteer

Support community growing sessions — watering, weeding, harvesting, helping new growers learn. Saturday mornings. One of the most peaceful and grounding volunteer roles in the programme.

Saturday mornings2–3 hrsOutdoors
🍎

Food Rescue Volunteer

Collect surplus food from supermarket and restaurant partners and bring it to the hub. Early mornings, flexible scheduling. Own transport needed. The work that makes everything else possible.

Early morningsFlexibleVehicle needed
👶

Holiday Club Volunteer

Help run the children's holiday food club — cooking with children, running growing activities, facilitating the daily lunch. Two-week programmes during each school holiday period. DBS check required.

School holidaysFull daysDBS check required
🏥

Welfare Visit Volunteer

Regular scheduled visits to isolated seniors or vulnerable individuals — not delivering food, but providing the human connection that is the companion to every meal delivery. An hour a week. An enormous difference.

Flexible scheduling1 hr/weekTraining provided
💻

Administrative & Digital Volunteer

Support the hub coordinator with records, communications, volunteer scheduling, grant reporting, and social media. Can be done remotely. Suits people with office, marketing, or administrative skills.

Flexible / remote2–4 hrs/weekAny location
What volunteering gives you

You give time. You receive more than you gave.

📚

Real food skills

Every kitchen session teaches a technique. Volunteers learn to cook well — naturally, without it feeling like a class.

🤝

Deep community connection

Volunteering with ZHIP connects you to your neighbourhood in a way few other activities do. You know your neighbours by name.

🏅

Volunteer certification

After 50 hours, you receive a ZHIP volunteer certificate — formally recognising your contribution in a way employers and professional bodies acknowledge.

❤️

The feeling of mattering

The elder who smiled when you arrived. The child who ate the meal you made. These are not small things. They are the point.

Getting started

From first contact to first session in one week.

1

Fill in the volunteer expression of interest

Tell us your name, location, availability, and which roles interest you. Takes five minutes. No commitment required at this stage.

2

We match you to a hub and a role

A ZHIP volunteer coordinator contacts you within 48 hours — matching you to the nearest hub and the role that best fits your skills and availability.

3

Complete the induction (2 hours)

Online or in person. Covers ZHIP values, your specific role, dignity-first service principles, basic food safety, and the ZHIP volunteer app. DBS check initiated at this stage for roles requiring one.

4

Attend your first session

You are paired with an experienced volunteer for your first two sessions. You observe, you help, you ask questions. By session three, you are contributing fully.
